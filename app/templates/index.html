Measurement methods & technical limits
Integrated LUFS, true peak and loudness range come from FFmpeg loudnorm (EBU R128 / BS.1770). RMS, sample peak, full-scale sample count, stereo energy balance and mean-centered stereo correlation use all decoded samples. Silence detection uses −50 dB for at least 0.5 seconds. Approximate dynamic range is the 95th-to-10th percentile ratio of active block RMS values, not a standardized DR score. Spectral metrics use Hann-windowed 2,048-sample FFTs sampled from evenly spaced blocks. Fingerprints cover at most the first 120 seconds. Mono balance and stereo correlation are unavailable. Silent signals may have no finite loudness or correlation. Reverb is a short multi-tap echo approximation. Analysis and fingerprints cannot predict proprietary copyright recognition.
